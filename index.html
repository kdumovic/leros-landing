About Leros

An Aegean hideaway most people never reach.

Leros sits in the Dodecanese, closer to Turkey than to mainland Greece — the beauty of the Mediterranean and the exclusivity of a private island, without the crowds of Mykonos or Santorini.

01

The Castle & the windmills

Climb to the Castle of the Virgin Mary, a Byzantine fortress over a thousand years old, for a 360-degree sunset over the sea, then enjoy a cocktail in a 17th-century windmill bar.

02

A beach for every mood

Step across the road and into the serene waters of Alinda Bay, the rumored bathing grounds of the Greek goddess Artemis, or "swim inside the color blue" in the calm shallows of secret beach Krifos.

03

Beach club days, taverna nights

Sink into a lounger for slow afternoons at the beach club, then gather for long taverna dinners of fresh-caught fish and local produce, lingering under the stars.

04

Storied history

Wander through Mussolini's forgotten Fascist utopia, descend into WWII tunnels carved deep into the mountains, and uncover the haunting tale of a shuttered asylum once called “Europe’s guilty secret.”

05

Island-hopping

Board a day boat to Patmos and its clifftop monastery where Saint John the Apostle wrote the Book of Revelation, or drift out to tiny, near-deserted islets where the only locals are a few friendly goats.

06

Eat and drink like a Greek Goddess

Wake to a freddo espresso on the beach each morning, and surrender your evenings to decadent, multi-course meals at Villa Betty and a gastronomic feast at famed Michelin-star restaurant Mylos by the Sea.

The cost

Everything handled, charged at cost.

Honest version: we've never run a trip like this before — it's an experiment, and we'd love for you to be part of it. So we've kept it simple. We organize and pay for everything up front, so you never reach for your credit card on the island. You settle up with us at cost — exactly what the week costs to put on — plus a flat 20% for the three of us planning and running every last detail. Nothing hidden, nothing padded beyond that.

Nothing's due yet. Once the group is set, we'll ask for a deposit before booking accommodation — and we'll show you the real, itemized numbers before you commit to anything. Fair and open, start to finish. Spots are limited.

Our estimate · per person
$2,500 & up

Our honest floor for a shared room — the best value, and the most fun way to do it. Your own private room runs a little more.

What moves the number

Two things: whether you share a room or want your own, and how many activities we plan for the group (and how far we roam). The estimate will firm up as guests confirm — always at cost, plus that same flat 20%.

What's covered

  • Seven nights' accommodation in Alinda, steps from the sea
  • All meals — taverna feasts, family dinners at the villa, snacks and drinks on hand, plus a private chef.
  • Drinks (spritzes, local wines & beers, ouzo) throughout the week.
  • Every activity & excursion: boat days, Patmos, watersports, classes, museums
  • All transport on the island — transfers, boats, the lot
  • Three hosts who've planned every detail so you don't have to

The only thing not included is getting to Leros. You book your own flights and ferries — and we'll walk you through exactly how. More on that below.

Getting to Leros

We'll help you get there.

Tell us your home airport and we'll personally map out the exact flights and ferries for you — and make it as easy as possible. Here are the main ways in:

By air

Fly via Athens

Into Athens, then a short domestic hop to Leros (LRS) with Olympic Air or Sky Express. Book early — the small planes fill up.

Athens → Leros · ~50 min
Island-hopper

Ferry via Kos or Rhodes

Fly to Kos (KOS) or Rhodes (RHO), then hop on a quick ferry to Leros. Easy to add a few extra Dodecanese days to either end of the trip.

Kos → Leros · ~1.5–2.5 hrs
Scenic

The overnight ferry

From Athens (Piraeus) straight to Leros — book a cabin and wake up in the Aegean. A few sailings a week.

Piraeus → Leros · ~9–10 hrs
Why we're doing this

We design experiences for brands. This one's for you.

Francesca and Kyle run an experience-design agency helping brands create unforgettable moments for their customers. Sergio is a top food-blogger and influencer in Europe, and plans high-end trips for a living. We've never done something like this together, for our friends or for anyone. This is the first, and it's for the people we love. It's for you to live like we live.

It's also an experiment. Francesca's island has been in her family for generations. Once you visit you'll understand why there's nowhere else like it. It's not a place you'd find on your own, it's inaccessible, it's difficult to reach. It's utterly devoid of tourists (which is part of why we love it!) but this makes it intimidating to plan a trip around. So instead we do all the planning. You just show up.

Last year, four of us. This year, twelve. Someday — fifty, a hundred. A yearly gathering of friends, maybe even a festival, in a beautiful corner of the world only a lucky few have discovered.

Leros is our blank canvas, and this trip is an experiment in what we can make it become. Join us this year and shape what comes next. That's our dream.
